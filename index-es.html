Cartography and geographic information systems

Production of cartographic outputs and spatial analyses for management, ecological land-use planning and environmental impact assessment.

  • Spatial analysis and geoprocessing applied to environmental assessment and land-use planning.
  • Integration and analysis of vector and raster information, including environmental and territorial data.
  • Calculation and analysis of spectral indices (NDVI, NDWI and derivatives).
  • Integration of artificial intelligence tools for classification, extraction and analysis of geospatial information.
  • Multicriteria analysis and territorial modeling to assess environmental suitability and compatibility.
  • Remote sensing and satellite-image interpretation to analyze land cover and environmental conditions.
  • Automation of geospatial processes using Graphical Modeler and Python/PyQGIS.
  • Management of reference systems, projections and transformations to integrate different cartographic sources.
